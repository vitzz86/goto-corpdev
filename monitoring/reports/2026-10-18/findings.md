## Useful examples checked against original sources

### 2026-01-27 · SEA food delivery
Momentum Works estimates 2025 regional platform food-delivery GMV at US$22.7 billion, up 18% year on year; ShopeeFood exceeded foodpanda in GMV. **Published research estimate; 2025 observations, not 2026 actuals.**

Analyst interpretation: compare affordability, order frequency and merchant-ad economics; do not apply a regional GMV ranking as Indonesia market share.

[Original source](https://thelowdown.momentum.asia/new-report-southeast-asia-food-delivery-gmv-grew-18-to-hit-us22-7b-in-2025/). Public report announcement reviewed; paid country tables not accessed.

### 2026-03-23 · Grab / foodpanda Taiwan
Grab announced an agreement to acquire foodpanda Taiwan for US$600 million cash, on a cash-free and debt-free basis, subject to adjustments and approvals. **Agreement announced; this review does not establish subsequent closing.**

Analyst interpretation: assess acquisition-led entry, integration costs, merchant retention and regulatory hurdles before importing regional expansion assumptions.

[Original source](https://www.grab.com/sg/press/others/grab-to-acquire-delivery-heros-foodpanda-delivery-business-in-taiwan/). Original transaction announcement reviewed.

### 2026-09-08 · foodpanda
foodpanda announced restaurant and meal discovery through ChatGPT and Claude; customers follow a link to the foodpanda app or website to order and pay. **Company-announced Singapore availability; no independently measured conversion uplift.**

Analyst interpretation: test conversational GoFood discovery with accurate menus, location, budget and dietary filters; measure referral conversion and acquisition cost.

[Original source](https://www.foodpanda.com/newsroom/foodpanda-brings-food-discovery-to-chatgpt-and-claude-in-singapore/). Original product announcement reviewed.

### 2026-09-15 · Grab / Atome Financial
Grab announced definitive agreements for a controlling 60% interest in Atome Financial for US$1.49 billion cash, including US$260 million of primary growth capital. **Proposed transaction; expected Phase 1 completion Q3 2027, subject to approvals and conditions.**

Analyst interpretation: monitor merchant distribution, underwriting and cross-sell against GoPay lending. Acquisition consideration, growth capital and total valuation must remain separate.

[Original source](https://www.grab.com/sg/press/others/grab-to-acquire-majority-stake-in-atome-financial-accelerating-growth-and-profitability-of-financial-services-segment/). Original issuer announcement and transaction terms reviewed.

### 2026-09-24 · Bank Indonesia / HKMA
The authorities signed an MoU to strengthen cooperation and explore interoperability for QR-based cross-border payments. **MoU and cooperation agenda; not evidence of a live interoperable consumer-payment launch.**

Analyst interpretation: track technical implementation, merchant acceptance, participating providers and settlement/FX economics before assigning GoPay volume.

[Original source](https://www.bi.go.id/id/publikasi/ruang-media/news-release/Pages/sp_2819426.aspx). Official central-bank announcement reviewed.

### 2026-09-30 · PT Credit Saison Indonesia Finance
OJK revoked this specific finance company’s business license under decree KEP-57/D.06/2026, effective on the decree date. **Revoked; exact Indonesian legal entity only.**

Analyst interpretation: monitor legal-entity-specific lender and partner licenses. Do not attribute this revocation to every Credit Saison group business or infer a GoTo exposure.

[Original source](https://ojk.go.id/id/berita-dan-kegiatan/pengumuman/Pages/OJK-Cabut-Izin-Usaha-Perusahaan-Pembiayaan-PT-Credit-Saison-Indonesia-Finance.aspx). Original Indonesian notice, dated release and later English page reconciled.

The original notice establishes the effective date as **24 September**. The Indonesian notice is dated **30 September**, while the English press page is dated **13 October**. Treating the English RSS date as a new October revocation would misdate the event.

### 2026-10-14 · Grab / OpenAI
Grab announced a Thailand AI training programme targeting more than 3,000 driver, delivery and merchant partners. **Training target; not a verified completed-training count or earnings uplift.**

Analyst interpretation: compare partner training plus in-app tools, using completion, active use and measured merchant/driver outcomes rather than enrolment targets.

[Original source](https://www.grab.com/th/en/press/others/grabxopenai/). Original country announcement reviewed.

## What the trial revealed

- GSM alone matched basketball game statistics. Green SM now requires transport/EV context, and those stale matches were removed from company attribution.
- Wallet searches also returned Dana White/UFC stories. Topic relevance now rejects those associations. Raw evidence stays in quarantine for audit.
- Broad native feeds and indexed searches contain off-topic technology, sports and other material; a successful feed response is not sufficient for publication.
- A 2026 article or report can describe 2025 financials. Publication date, event date and reporting period remain separate.
- Country and legal-entity scope still require editorial review; a Vietnam Grab headline does not establish an Indonesia operating effect.
